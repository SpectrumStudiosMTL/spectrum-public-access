FROM INSIDE FILMLAB

There's a lot more once you join

FilmLab is Spectrum's home for young filmmakers and animators. A free account gets you a portfolio, the full Watch List, and your own home base — no program enrollment needed.

Portfolio

BUILD YOUR PORTFOLIO

Your bio, your film credits, your artist statement — one growing portfolio, private by default, that you can also claim as your own shareable website once it's ready to show off.

 Community

EXPLORE THE GALLERY

See portfolios from other young filmmakers and animators who've chosen to share their work with the community.
